HELP & ANSWERS

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Everything you need to know about combining networks, device tethering, and download speeds.

How does Pulse make downloads faster? ➔
Pulse splits your download into smaller chunks, downloads different chunks simultaneously over all the network connections you've enabled (such as your home Wi-Fi, Ethernet, and phone USB tethering), then puts them back together into a single finished file on disk. This lets one download use the combined bandwidth capacity of all your available connections.
What network connections can I combine? ➔
You can combine any active network adapters detected on your computer! For example, your broadband Wi-Fi, a wired Gigabit Ethernet cable, a 4G or 5G smartphone connected through USB tethering, or secondary USB Wi-Fi dongles. Each connection simply needs to provide a separate route to the internet.
Why does Windows SmartScreen show an unrecognized app warning? ➔
Commercial EV (Extended Validation) code-signing certificates cost hundreds of dollars every year. Because Pulse is 100% free software, it is not EV signed yet. Windows may display an "Unrecognized app" warning the first time you run Pulse. Follow the first-launch steps: click "More info" and then "Run anyway". You only have to do this once, and the installer is completely clean, virus-free, and contains zero adware.
Does Pulse act as a VPN? ➔
No. Pulse combines connections strictly for downloads started inside Pulse. It isn't a VPN, installs zero virtual network drivers, and doesn't change how your web browser, online games, Discord, or video streams access the internet. All other applications continue using your system's default network route normally.
How do I use my Android or iPhone over USB? ➔
Plug your phone into your computer using a standard USB cable. On Android: open Settings → Hotspot & Tethering → Turn on USB Tethering. On iPhone: open Settings → Personal Hotspot → Allow Others to Join and connect via USB. Windows will instantly recognize it as a separate Ethernet network adapter, and Pulse will automatically bind it into your download queue.
